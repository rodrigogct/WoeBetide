UNDERSTANDING COLLECTION PERFORMANCE

CAPITAL RECOVERY

Measures how much of the original collection investment has been recovered through sales revenue.

SELL-THROUGH

Measures how quickly a collection is being converted from inventory into sold garments.

REALIZED ROI

Measures the gross return on garments already sold. It excludes remaining unsold inventory and operating expenses.
